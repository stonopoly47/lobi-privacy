Lobi Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026. Lobi is a small app for watching films together. It has no accounts and no ads.

What Lobi handles

  • The name and face you pick. They are saved on your phone and shown to the other people in the same cinema session.
  • Chat messages and reactions you send. They go only to people in the same session and are not stored by us.
  • The join code and your network address, which are needed to connect you to the host through a relay service (Unity Relay).

What Lobi does not do

Lobi does not use your camera, microphone, location, contacts or files. It does not show ads, sell data or track you across apps.

Third parties

Connections run through Unity Relay, a service provided by Unity Technologies, which sees connection details such as IP addresses while a session is active.

Your choices

Uninstalling the app removes the name and face saved on your phone. Nothing about you is kept on a Lobi server.
